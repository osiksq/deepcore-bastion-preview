Original combat samples — Deepcore Bastion

pulse-1/2/3, hit-1/2, armor-hit, death-1/2, missile-launch,
explosion, saw-impact and arc-impact are original deterministic synthesis.
They contain no external recordings or sample-pack material. Their recipes
are stored in scripts/combat-synthesis.mjs and rendered offline through
scripts/render-combat-sfx.mjs. LicenseRef-Original-Project in manifest.json
identifies this project-origin material, not a third-party license grant.
No external attribution obligation applies to these original samples.

The retained interface, reward and warning cues use the Kenney sources below.
See manifest.json for per-file provenance, processing and content hashes.
